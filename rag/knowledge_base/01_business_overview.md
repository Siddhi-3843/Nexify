# Nexify Business Overview

## What is Nexify?
Nexify is an AI-powered business intelligence platform for everyday life businesses including fitness clubs, gyms, childcare centers, recreation centers, golf clubs, and field service businesses.

## Business Model
- Monthly subscription memberships
- Three tiers: Basic ($29/month), Standard ($59/month), Premium ($99/month)
- Revenue comes from recurring monthly payments

## Key Business Facts
- Average gym has 300-600 active members
- Healthy monthly churn rate is between 2-5%
- January is the highest signup month (New Year resolutions)
- Summer months (June-August) see 15% drop in attendance
- Peak hours are 6-8am and 5-7pm on weekdays
- Average member lifetime is 14 months
- Premium members churn 40% less than basic members

## Business Goals
- Increase member retention
- Maximize Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Improve class attendance rates
- Reduce payment failures
- Identify at-risk members early
