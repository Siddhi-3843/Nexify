# Member Retention Playbook

## Early Warning Signs of Churn
1. Member misses 2 consecutive weeks of classes
2. Payment failure occurs
3. Booking frequency drops by 50% compared to previous month
4. Member downgrades from premium/standard to basic tier
5. Member stops responding to emails

## Retention Actions by Risk Level

### Low Risk Members (Risk Score 0-30)
- Send monthly newsletter with class highlights
- Offer loyalty rewards after 6 months
- Personalized class recommendations

### Medium Risk Members (Risk Score 31-60)
- Send personal check-in email from manager
- Offer one free personal training session
- Invite to exclusive member events
- Survey to understand satisfaction

### High Risk Members (Risk Score 61-100)
- Immediate personal phone call
- Offer 1 month free or 50% discount
- Urgent re-engagement email campaign
- Assign dedicated member success manager

## Best Email Send Times
- Tuesday and Thursday mornings (10am-11am)
- Avoid Mondays and Fridays
- Avoid weekends for professional communication

## Proven Re-engagement Messages
- "We miss you! Here's a free class on us"
- "Your favourite instructor asked about you"
- "New classes added — perfect for your fitness goals"
- "Exclusive member discount — this week only"

## Payment Failure Recovery
1. Retry payment automatically after 3 days
2. Send friendly payment reminder email
3. If still failed after 7 days — personal phone call
4. Offer alternative payment method
5. If failed after 14 days — pause membership (don't cancel)
