# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Revenue Metrics

### MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- Definition: Total revenue collected in a single month
- Formula: Sum of all active member monthly fees
- Example: 368 active members × average $45/month = $16,560 MRR
- Good MRR growth: 5-10% month over month

### ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- Definition: Average amount each member pays per month
- Formula: MRR ÷ number of active members
- Nexify benchmark: $45-65 per member per month

### LTV (Lifetime Value)
- Definition: Total revenue from one member over their entire membership
- Formula: ARPU × average member lifespan in months
- Example: $45 × 14 months = $630 LTV per member

## Member Metrics

### Churn Rate
- Definition: Percentage of members who cancelled in a given month
- Formula: Members cancelled ÷ total members at start of month × 100
- Healthy churn rate: 2-5% per month
- Danger zone: above 8% per month
- Our current churn: 132 churned out of 500 = 26.4% total historical churn

### Retention Rate
- Definition: Percentage of members who stayed
- Formula: 100% - Churn Rate
- Target retention rate: 95% or higher per month

### Attendance Rate
- Definition: Percentage of bookings where member actually showed up
- Formula: Attended bookings ÷ total bookings × 100
- Healthy attendance rate: 75-85%

## Payment Metrics

### Payment Failure Rate
- Definition: Percentage of payments that failed
- Formula: Failed payments ÷ total payments × 100
- Our current rate: 211 failed out of 7483 = 2.8% (healthy!)
- Action needed if above 5%
